## Overview

MEX Dashboards brings together the outputs of every other MEX system — thermal alerts from HeatWatch, yield trends from Vision Systems, energy data from EMS, and predictions from Digital Twin — into a single, coherent interface that operators and managers actually want to use.

## Design Philosophy

We designed Dashboards with two constraints: it must be usable by a plant operator who has never seen a software dashboard before, and it must be powerful enough for a process engineer doing root-cause analysis. The result is a layered UI: simple overview screens for the control room, deep drill-downs for engineers.
