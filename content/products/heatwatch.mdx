## Overview

HeatWatch brings professional-grade thermal surveillance to Indian manufacturing facilities at a fraction of the cost of traditional handheld thermal cameras or outsourced thermal inspection rounds. By converting reactive maintenance into continuous predictive intelligence, plants using HeatWatch detect abnormal thermal gradients early before they cause unplanned downtime.

## How It Works

The system mounts in a fixed position overlooking the asset under watch. The edge computing unit processes every frame locally, running MEX's proprietary thermal anomaly models. No raw video or sensitive process data ever leaves the plant floor.
