## Built for the plant floor

HeatWatch is designed for 24/7 operation. It recovers automatically from power cuts and network interruptions, keeps its data locally, and needs no operator action to start. Plant teams see temperatures, alarm states and trends on a single screen, and quality teams get audit-ready records without manual logging.

## Integration

HeatWatch connects to industrial RTD modules over standard protocols and runs alongside your existing control system. It doesn't require any changes to your PLC or SCADA.
