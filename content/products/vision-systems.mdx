## Overview

Manual visual inspection is the most common quality gate in Indian manufacturing — and the least reliable. Inspector fatigue, shift changes, lighting variation, and subjective judgement all introduce variability. MEX Vision Systems eliminate that variability with machine-consistent, millisecond-speed decisions.
