howItWorks: "TwinForge Studio runs a physics model of the process skid, covering flows, levels, temperatures and valve travel, on a fixed scan cycle. Test scenarios apply inputs and faults step by step, check the PLC's response against the specification, and produce a signed-off FAT report."
