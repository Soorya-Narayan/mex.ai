## Overview

Most industrial facilities are a patchwork of equipment from different eras — a 1990s PLC here, a modern servo drive there, an analogue instrument that only speaks 4–20 mA. MEX Data Logger is designed to bridge this heterogeneous reality into a single, coherent, time-synchronised data stream.

## Deployment Topology

The Data Logger can operate standalone or as part of a hierarchical MEX system where multiple field loggers feed into a site-level historian, which in turn feeds MEX Dashboards and the Digital Twin.
