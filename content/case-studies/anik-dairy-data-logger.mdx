## Overview

Implemented at **Anik Dairy, Bhopal**, logging around 30 instruments on a 10 KL pasteuriser, including temperature, pressure and flow transmitters, flow diversion valves, and CIP status.

## System Setup

- **PLC Connection:** Allen-Bradley Micro800 series over EtherNet/IP, configured through a tag map with no PLC code changes
- **Sampling & Storage:** 1-second sampling written in flash-safe batches to a local SQLite database (WAL mode) on a Raspberry Pi edge computer
- **Dashboard & Reports:** Browser-based supervisory dashboard (3-second live refresh) with multi-axis trend charts, plus automatic shift (8 h) and daily reports in PDF, Excel, and CSV
