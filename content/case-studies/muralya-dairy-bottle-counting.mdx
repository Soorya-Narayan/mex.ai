## Overview

Implemented at **Muralya Dairy, Thiruvananthapuram**, for detecting and counting milk bottles in real time on the production conveyor.

## System Setup

- **Detection Model:** YOLOv8n (3M parameters), exported to ONNX (11.8 MB) and PyTorch
- **Site-Specific Training:** Trained on on-site images captured at Muralya Dairy's line and annotated in-house
- **Operator View:** Real-time bottle detection on the live camera feed with on-screen count overlay and live count trend graph
