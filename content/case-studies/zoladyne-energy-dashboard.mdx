## Overview

Built for **Zoladyne, Coimbatore**, as a real-time energy monitoring dashboard covering solar production, grid usage, battery health, and load consumption.

## System Capabilities

- **Views:** Summary, Solar, Grid, and Load views with animated power flow between solar, grid, battery, and load
- **Monitored Metrics:** Power (kW), energy (kWh), voltage (V), current (A), grid frequency (Hz), power factor, battery state of charge and health, and solar inverter MPPT status and efficiency
- **Reporting & Assistant:** Line, pie, and bar charts with drill-down breakdowns; PDF, Excel, CSV, and PNG exports; and a built-in assistant that answers plain-language questions on battery status and solar generation
