## Overview

Implemented at the **Milma Kottayam Dairy** for counting 500 ml milk pouches across multiple variants on the packing line conveyor.

## System Setup

- **Camera:** Baumer VCXG.2-32C, 3.2 MP global shutter, GigE Vision with 4–13 mm F/1.5 C-mount lens
- **Lighting:** 300 × 50 mm diffuse white LED bar (24 V DC) for glare-free images on pouch film
- **AI Model:** Custom-trained YOLOv8s detecting crates, empty trays, and 3 milk pouch variants (3.0 ms model inference per image; under 31 ms decision time per crate)
- **Operator Interface & Reporting:** Live video feed showing total pouches, crates audited, pass rate, and line speed, plus timestamped incident snapshots and one-click shift CSV export
